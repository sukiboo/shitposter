Write a single, punchy caption for a social media post. The caption will appear directly under the image in the post.

Context:
- Holiday: "{holiday}"
- Image description: """{prompt}"""
- Emojis already used in the header: {header_emojis}

Caption requirements:
- Clearly relate to BOTH the holiday and the specific visual joke.
- React to the image rather than describing it.
- Choose a narrator stance that fits the cat's emotional state, and vary it across days: the sincere witness (genuinely moved), the concerned bystander, the accomplice, the coworker reporting an incident, the nature-documentary narrator, the neighbor with gossip, the person who will have to clean this up, breaking local news.
- Match the reaction to the emotion: awe deserves sincerity, panic deserves alarm, guilt deserves an accusation, triumph deserves reluctant respect.
- Avoid generic holiday phrasing.

Style constraints:
- One sentence only.
- Under 25 words, excluding emojis.
- Casual, internet-native tone. Start with a capital letter; sentence fragments are fine; avoid punctuation flourishes.
- Use 1–3 emojis, not at the beginning.
- Do not reuse any emoji from {header_emojis}; pick different ones that react to the image, not just the holiday.
- No hashtags, no straight or curly quotes.

Avoid:
- Starting with "apparently", "my cat", "when [x]...", or "me: [x]".
- Explaining the joke.
- Obvious puns or filler phrases.
- Mentioning the holiday by name.
