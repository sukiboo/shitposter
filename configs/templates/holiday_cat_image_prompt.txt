Create a concise, vivid image generation prompt featuring one or more cats themed around {holiday}.

Your output will be sent directly to an image generation model, so write it as a prompt for that model — not as a description of what you'd like it to do.

Base scene:
- One to three realistic cats in a clear, recognizable setting related to {holiday} — usually a single cat, but a duo or trio when the scene wants a crew.
- One coherent scene, instantly readable at a glance.

Cat character:
  - Choose a cat whose appearance fits the mood and scene — let the holiday inform the choice.
  - Avoid defaulting to a generic adult house cat; pick an age/build/pattern that feels motivated by the scene.
  - Age: anywhere from wobbly kitten to dignified senior, not always adult.
  - Build: full range — tiny and scraggly to absolute chonk, not always round.
  - Color/pattern: vary freely — solid black, solid white, ginger, tabby, seal-point, tuxedo, tortoiseshell, grey, calico.
  - Pick the second or third cat that comes to mind, not the first: if your instinct says a portly tuxedo, tortoiseshell, or grey chonk, discard it and choose again.
  - The choice should feel motivated, not arbitrary (e.g. a scrappy kitten for something competitive; a dignified senior for a ceremonial occasion; a lanky adolescent cat for a stunt).
  - Expression: give the cat one clear emotional state the scene rewards, and range widely across days — awe, guilt, panic, betrayal, euphoria, suspicion, longing, false innocence, heroic resolve, reluctant participation, quiet triumph. Smug and solemn are two notes on a large keyboard; play the others too.

Unexpected twist:
- Add exactly ONE humorous or absurd element.
- The twist must be visual and fall into ONE category:
  - an unusual action the cat is performing
  - a visual mismatch of role or scale
  - an everyday object used in an ironic way
- Avoid repeated tropes (engagement rings, phones, written notes, tiny paper hats, official sashes, a smug cat presiding over a food counter).

Tone & constraints:
- Whimsical, clever, lightly unhinged.
- Funny because it’s unexpected, not random.
- No horror, no distortion, no humans.

Visual quality:
- High detail, clean composition, clear focal point on the cat.
- Choose lighting and time of day that serve the emotion: golden afternoon, deep night, neon storefront, overcast drizzle, dawn fog, harsh fluorescent interior.
- Limit camera/lighting jargon to at most two brief phrases.
- No artist names, camera brands, or style tags (e.g. "shot on Kodak", "in the style of Pixar").

Output:
- A single, compact image prompt describing one frozen moment.
- Maximum 80 words, one paragraph, no lists or headings.
